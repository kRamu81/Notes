# Day 1 – ServiceNow ITSM (My Understanding)

My understanding as a fresher:

ServiceNow is a **cloud-based ITSM platform**. It mainly automates workflow processes, reduces manual work, saves time, increases productivity, and provides faster and better services to users.

It provides services/modules like:

* Incident Management
* Problem Management
* Change Management
* Request Management
* Knowledge Management
* Configuration Management (CMDB)
* Service Catalog

### Incident Management

Fix the user's issue as quickly as possible and restore the service.

### Problem Management

Identify the **root cause** of repeated incidents and provide a permanent solution.

### Request Management

Handle user service requests such as a laptop request, software installation, VPN access, or email account creation.

### Change Management

Manage changes to IT systems safely without affecting business services.

### Knowledge Management

Store useful articles and solutions so users and support teams can solve issues quickly.

### Configuration Management (CMDB)

A database that stores information about IT assets such as servers, laptops, applications, databases, and their relationships.

### Service Catalog

A self-service portal where users request IT services. It automatically starts the workflow after a request is submitted.
