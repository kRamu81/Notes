# Generative AI - Notes

## 1. What is Generative AI?

Generative AI became widely popular around 2021 and continues to grow rapidly.

### Impact of Generative AI

* It can automate or assist with around 10% of tasks performed by more than 80% of workers.
* It is expected to contribute significantly to global GDP growth in the coming years.

### Definition

Generative AI refers to AI systems that can create high-quality content such as:

* Text
* Images
* Audio
* Video
* Code

### Popular Generative AI Tools

* ChatGPT – OpenAI
* Bard (now Gemini) – Google
* Microsoft Copilot (formerly Bing Chat)

### Current Uses of AI Technology

* Web Search (Google, Bing)
* Fraud Detection (Credit Card Transactions)
* Chatbots and Virtual Assistants
* Content Generation (Text, Images, Videos, Audio)

Unlike traditional AI systems that mainly analyze data, Generative AI creates new content.

---

## 2. How Does Generative AI Work?

Generative AI is part of a broader set of AI technologies and tools.

### Supervised Learning

Supervised learning is a machine learning technique where:

* Models are trained using labeled data.
* The system learns the relationship between inputs and outputs.
* The model predicts results based on patterns learned from the data.

### Large Language Models (LLMs)

Generative text systems use Large Language Models (LLMs).

Examples:

* ChatGPT
* Gemini
* Claude

### Text Generation Process

LLMs are initially trained using supervised learning.

The model learns by repeatedly predicting the next word in a sequence.

Example:

Input:

> Artificial Intelligence is

Prediction:

> transforming

The model continues predicting one word after another to generate complete sentences and paragraphs.

---

## 3. LLMs as a Thought Partner

Large Language Models can act as a thought partner and assist with many tasks.

### Information Assistant

LLMs help users:

* Find information
* Answer questions
* Research topics
* Summarize content

Examples:

* ChatGPT
* Gemini
* Claude

### Writing Assistant

LLMs can:

* Rewrite text
* Explain concepts
* Improve grammar
* Generate ideas
* Create summaries
* Draft emails and reports

Unlike traditional web search, LLMs can generate, explain, and interact with information.

---

## 4. AI as a General-Purpose Technology

AI is considered a general-purpose technology because it can support a wide variety of tasks.

### Examples

* Reading and summarizing documents
* Writing content
* Chatbots and customer support
* Coding assistance
* Data analysis
* Image and video generation

---

## 5. Types of AI Applications

### A. Web-Based Applications

These are accessed through a web browser.

Examples:

* ChatGPT
* Google Gemini
* Microsoft Copilot

### B. Software-Based Applications

AI is integrated directly into software systems.

Examples:

* Email routing and classification
* Fraud detection systems
* Recommendation engines
* Customer service automation

---

## Key Takeaways

* Generative AI creates new content such as text, images, audio, video, and code.
* Large Language Models (LLMs) power most modern generative AI applications.
* LLMs generate text by predicting the next word repeatedly.
* AI can act as an information assistant and writing partner.
* AI is a general-purpose technology used across many industries.
* AI applications can be web-based or integrated into software systems.
