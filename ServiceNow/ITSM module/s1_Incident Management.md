# ServiceNow ITSM and Incident Management Notes

## Introduction to ITSM

ITSM (IT Service Management) is the practice of managing and delivering IT services to users and businesses.

ServiceNow provides a cloud-based ITSM platform that helps organizations manage IT services efficiently.

### Benefits of ServiceNow ITSM

* Automation: Reduces manual work by automating processes.
* Reporting: Generates reports to track performance and trends.
* Centralized Platform: Manages IT activities in one place.

### Main ITSM Modules

* Incident Management
* Problem Management
* Change Management
* Asset Management
* Service Catalog
* Knowledge Management

# Incident Management

## What is an Incident?

An incident is an unplanned interruption to an IT service or a reduction in the quality of an IT service.

### Examples

* Email is not working.
* Internet connection is slow.
* Printer is not responding.
* User cannot log in to the system.

---

## What is Incident Management?

Incident Management is the process of restoring normal service as quickly as possible and minimizing business impact.

### Objectives

* Restore services quickly.
* Track and manage incidents.
* Assign incidents to the correct team.
* Resolve incidents within SLA timelines.
* Keep users informed about progress.

---

## Benefits of Incident Management

### For Users

* Faster issue resolution.
* Better communication and updates.

### For Organizations

* Reduced downtime.
* Improved efficiency.
* Better service quality.

---

# Incident Management Process

1. Incident occurs.
2. Incident is reported.
3. Incident is logged.
4. Incident is assigned to the appropriate team.
5. Investigation and diagnosis are performed.
6. The issue is resolved and service is restored.
7. The incident is closed.

---

# Incident States

* New – Incident is created.
* In Progress – Work has started.
* On Hold – Waiting for information or another team.
* Resolved – Solution has been provided.
* Closed – Incident is officially completed.
* Canceled – Invalid or duplicate incident.

---

# Important Points to Remember

* Incident = Restore service quickly.
* ServiceNow helps automate the incident process.
* SLA defines the expected resolution time.
* Users receive updates throughout the incident lifecycle.
