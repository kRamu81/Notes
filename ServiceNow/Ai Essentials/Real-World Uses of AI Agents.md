# Real-World Uses of AI Agents – Course Notes

---

## Introduction

We are at the **beginning of the agentic AI era**.
AI agents have huge potential to assist humans at work — and they are still growing and evolving.

> Key point: AI agents **support people** — they do not replace them.

---

## AI Agent Use Cases by Workflow

---

### 1. IT Workflows
- **Monitor systems** 24/7 and detect issues before they become outages
- **Auto-resolve** common IT tickets (password resets, access requests)
- **Generate reports** on system performance and incidents
- **Triage and route** complex issues to the right IT team automatically

---

### 2. HR Workflows
- **Onboarding new employees** — automates document collection, system access, and task assignments
- **Answer HR questions** — policies, leave balances, benefits (like a smart HR chatbot)
- **Screen job applications** — filters candidates based on criteria
- **Track and remind** employees about training deadlines or compliance requirements

---

### 3. Customer Workflows
- **Handle customer queries** automatically — returns, order status, complaints
- **Personalize responses** based on customer history
- **Escalate complex issues** to a human agent with full context ready
- **Follow up** with customers automatically after service interactions

---

### 4. Security Workflows
- **Monitor network activity** in real time for threats
- **Detect anomalies** and flag suspicious behavior instantly
- **Respond to incidents** automatically — isolate affected systems, alert teams
- **Generate security reports** and track compliance status

---

### 5. Software Development
- **Write and review code** based on requirements
- **Run automated tests** and catch bugs before deployment
- **Document code** automatically
- **Suggest improvements** based on coding patterns and best practices

---

### 6. Finance and Supply Chain
- **Detect fraudulent transactions** by analyzing spending patterns
- **Automate invoice processing** and approvals
- **Monitor supply chain** for delays or disruptions and suggest alternatives
- **Forecast demand** and optimize inventory levels automatically

---

## Why This Matters

AI agents are transforming work across **every major industry** — but always as a **partner to humans**, not a replacement.

| What AI Agents Do | What Humans Do |
|---|---|
| Handle routine, repetitive tasks | Focus on complex decisions |
| Process and analyze large data fast | Set goals and priorities |
| Work 24/7 without breaks | Provide judgment and creativity |
| Learn and improve over time | Guide, correct, and improve AI |

---

## Key Takeaways

- AI agents are being used across **IT, HR, Customer Service, Security, Dev, and Finance**
- In every case, they **support humans** — not replace them
- They handle the **routine work** so people can focus on **higher-value tasks**
- We are still at the **early stage** — the potential is much bigger going forward
- The more AI agents are used, the **smarter and more capable** they become
