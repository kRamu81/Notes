# Workflow Automation & AI Agents – Course Notes

---

## What is a Workflow?

A workflow is a **series of steps** to complete a task — consistently and in order.

**Everyday example — Making Dinner:**

**1.** Choose a recipe
**2.** Get ingredients from the fridge
**3.** Prep — chop, measure, warm the pan
**4.** Cook the ingredients
**5.** Plate the food
**6.** Eat and clean up

Professional workflows work the same way — pulling reports, routing tasks, scheduling handoffs, approvals, and more.

---

## What is Workflow Automation?

Workflow automation means **organizing and automating tasks** in a workflow so that:
- Work gets done **faster and more accurately**
- **Delays are eliminated**
- The right work goes to the **right people at the right time**

**SaaS platforms** like ServiceNow's **Now Platform** help route work to the right systems and people automatically.

---

## How AI Agents Fit Into Workflows

AI agents act like **digital coworkers** — they take on tasks independently so humans can focus on more complex work.

They use:
- **Data** — to understand what needs to be done
- **Decision-making models** — to figure out the best way to do it

---

## How AI Agents Are Set Up in a Workflow

### 4 Key Components:

**1. Use Case**
- Tell the **LLM (Large Language Model)** what you need done
- The LLM acts as the **orchestrator** — it plans and directs the agents

**2. Tools**
- AI agents need access to **technologies and resources** to do their tasks
- These are their tools (apps, databases, systems, APIs)

**3. Orchestration**
- The orchestrator **plans, reasons, and assigns tasks** to the right agents
- Multiple agents work **together in harmony** to achieve the goal

**4. Scale**
- There is **no limit** to the number of tasks that can be automated
- Easily **add more agents** to handle more complex or high-volume work

## Where AI Agents Add the Most Value

| Best Use | Why |
|---|---|
| Repetitive tasks | Same steps every time — perfect for automation |
| Routine processes | No complex judgment needed |
| High-volume requests | Agents scale easily to handle more |

## Key Takeaways

- A **workflow** is a set of steps to complete a task consistently
- **Workflow automation** removes delays and ensures accuracy
- AI agents work as **digital coworkers** — handling tasks so humans focus on bigger work
