# Introduction to Generative AI – Course Notes

---

## AI Terms – Quick Definitions

| Term | Simple Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** | Making machines think and act like humans |
| **Machine Learning (ML)** | Machines learn from data to make decisions |
| **Deep Learning** | Advanced ML using layers of algorithms (like the human brain) |
| **Prompt Engineering** | Writing good instructions to get better AI outputs |

---

## What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of AI that **creates new content** — text, images, audio, video, or code — by learning patterns from existing data.

It can:
- Have human-like conversations
- Resolve issues and answer questions
- Offload routine/repetitive tasks
- Generate content on demand

---

## What is Multimodal AI?

Multimodal AI can **accept many types of input** at the same time — text, audio, video, images — and give output in multiple formats.

**Example:** Ask for a soup recipe → AI gives you text, a video, and a podcast all at once.

> Previous AI: one input type → one output type
> Multimodal AI: many input types → many output types = **more accurate results**

---

## How Does Generative AI Work?

### Step 1 – Training
- The AI is fed **huge amounts of data** (text, images, videos, etc.)
- It makes **millions of practice predictions** to learn patterns
- Uses **RLHF** (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback) — AI is **rewarded** for outputs humans prefer
- RLHF is an **ongoing process** — constantly improving

### Step 2 – Tuning (Fine-tuning)
- AI is guided on **how to format and present** its output
- Example: make the image look illustrated, or reply in a formal tone

### Step 3 – Generation
- AI creates **brand new content** based on what it learned
- There is no single "right answer" — output resembles training data but is unique
- New outputs are **evaluated** and used to improve future results

---

## Benefits of Generative AI

- **Cost savings** — automates repetitive tasks, reduces manual work
- **Better customer service** — faster, 24/7 support
- **Targeted marketing** — personalized content for users
- **Higher revenue** — faster product development and innovation

---

## Limitations of Generative AI

- **Struggles with new situations** — may not adapt well outside its training data
- **Accuracy issues** — can produce wrong or inappropriate content
- **Lack of transparency** — hard to explain *why* it gave a certain answer ("black box")

---

## Risks of Generative AI

🚩 **Harmful bias** — AI may carry biases from training data into its outputs

🚩 **Intellectual property** — AI may use copyrighted content without permission; your data may become part of its training

🚩 **Cybersecurity** — criminals use AI to attack systems and steal sensitive data

🚩 **Misinformation & fakes** — AI can create false or misleading content (deepfakes, fake news)

🚩 **Environmental cost** — AI data centers use huge amounts of electricity and water

---

## Key Takeaways

- **AI → ML → Deep Learning → Generative AI** (each builds on the previous)
- Generative AI **creates new content** by learning from existing data
- It works in 3 steps: **Train → Tune → Generate**
- **RLHF** is how AI learns what "good" looks like from humans
- Gen AI has huge **benefits** but also real **risks** — organizations must manage both
- **Multimodal AI** = smarter AI that uses multiple data types together
