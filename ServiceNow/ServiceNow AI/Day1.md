### ServiceNow AI

- ServiceNow is using AI features.
- AI helps users complete work faster and automate tasks.

### Types of AI

**Generative AI**
- Generative AI is used to generate something.
- Example:Text,Image,Video,Code,Summary

**Agentic AI**
- Agentic AI can do actions based on the given task.
- It can understand the task, make decisions, and complete the work automatically.


### AI Features Used in ServiceNow

### 1. Now Assist
- Now Assist is an AI module in ServiceNow.
- It is the place where we can start the AI journey in ServiceNow.
- It uses Generative AI.
- It helps generate summaries, responses, knowledge articles, and other content.

### 2. AI Search
- AI Search helps users find records and information quickly.
- It understands the user's question and gives better search results.

### 3. Predictive Intelligence
- Predictive Intelligence uses Machine Learning.
- It predicts values based on previous data.
- Example: Auto Assignment , Category Prediction , Priority Prediction

### Now Assist Admin

- Open **Now Assist Admin**.
- Go to **Skills**.
- Choose the management/application.
- Install the required plugin from the ServiceNow Store.
- Activate the skill.

Example:
- ITSM has around **13 AI skills** available (depends on the ServiceNow release).
