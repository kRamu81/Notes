# Activity 1 – Create the Application Framework

## Scenario
You are a **Business Process Analyst** at Cloud Dimensions.
Need an app to **log and track safety issues** — but IT is busy for 6+ months.
Solution: use **App Engine Studio** — no coding needed!

---

## Steps

**1.** Log into your **Now Learning** instance.

**2.** Go to **All menu** → search `engine`

**3.** Select **App Engine → App Engine Studio**
<img width="802" height="670" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b3297d05-85db-4d80-bb60-1a87e1d80de6" />


**4.** Click **Get Started** on the welcome dialog box.
<img width="1680" height="1247" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3b0c4d66-5035-4456-a444-31beee4c3a44" />



**5.** Click **Create app** (top right of AES homepage)
<img width="1680" height="543" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a57ebc84-68ba-41f2-9946-726021df0cf6" />


**6.** Fill in the app details:
- **Name:** Safety
- **Description:** This application allows users to log and track Safety Issues.
- **Logo:** Upload `safetylogo.png`

**7.** Click **Continue**
<img width="1680" height="734" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ac75b086-ac90-4f90-af38-dc19a3b3074c" />


**8.** Keep the default **Admin** and **User** roles — no changes needed.

**9.** Click **Continue**
<img width="1680" height="808" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aaa8e5f6-6fbf-457f-b9cf-bf2f4658bdb5" />


**10.** Click **Go to app dashboard** ✓
<img width="1680" height="780" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e254444e-be7f-44c9-b8d8-58277dff61c9" />


---

## Key Takeaways
- AES lets you build apps **without any code**
- Always set a clear **name, description, and roles**
- Default roles (admin + user) are enough to get started
